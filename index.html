How to play: click your Pokémon → click a blue tile to move (click its own tile to stay) → Attack or Wait. Moves work like weapons: each has power, accuracy, crit, range and PP (uses). Click an enemy while moving to walk next to it and open the forecast; switch moves with ◀ ▶ and targets with ▲ ▼. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter/Z selects, Esc/X goes back. Damage comes from the physical/special split (Atk vs Def, SpA vs SpD). Type matchups work like FE's weapon triangle: super-effective +20 Hit and +10% damage (×4: +40 Hit, +25%), resisted −20 Hit and −10% (×¼: −40, −25%), immune can't hit. Same-type moves get +15 Hit. Hit % shown is the true chance (FE-style two-roll average, so 80+ almost always lands). A Pokémon counters with its equipped move (the last one it used, marked E) when it can reach. Units 5+ Spe faster strike twice. Click an enemy to see its range. Esc / right-click cancels.
Fainted Pokémon leave the battle but come back next chapter. Oran Trees heal 20% at the start of your phase. Water-types swim, Grass-types move freely through forest, Rock/Ground climb mountains, Flying-types ignore all terrain and can stop over water.
